About Our PTO

Bringing families, teachers, and staff together.

The Moore Elementary PTO supports students and staff by coordinating volunteers, assisting with school-hosted events, and helping create a welcoming, connected school community.

Everything we do is powered by volunteers and community support. From Moore Miles and Book Fair to teacher appreciation and classroom celebrations, family participation helps make a real difference. All parents and guardians of Moore Elementary students are automatically members of the PTO.

Whether you can help with one event, contribute behind the scenes, or stay involved throughout the year — there’s a place for every Moore family. As an FSD school PTO, our role is to assist Moore Elementary with volunteers and family engagement. Gifts and donations are made to the school, and PTO representatives serve on the School Advisory Team to help discuss priorities for funds raised through school-hosted events.
